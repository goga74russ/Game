## Концепты зон (изображение, 16:9)
Общий хвост для всех (добавлять в конец): `painterly photorealism, dark fantasy Slavic myth, dry thunderstorm with no rain, dust and static in the air, pale blue-violet lightning without white core, saturation below 60 percent, 16:9, no text`.

**Зона 1 — Сухоречье (концепт, keyframe):**
```
Concept art, environment keyframe. A small highland village in a wide dry valley at warm early morning. Seven low log houses of weathered grey timber with sloped sod roofs of dry yellowed turf, dark carved oak window frames with six-petal thunder rosettes, closed doors. A dry cracked clay riverbed runs past the village. A wooden well sweep with an empty wooden bucket on a chain in the center. Wicker fences between yards, long benches by the walls with faded old men sitting on them. A tall wooden pole beside every house, iron cables twisted with dried sinew rising from the ground at its base, wires stretching uphill to the north. Far up the slope on the ridge, a single colossal old oak bound with iron hoops, a thunderhead on the upper left. Long warm slanting shadows, amber light. Calm, lived-in, quietly wrong.
```
**Околица (концепт):**
```
Concept art, environment. A narrow path leaving a highland village uphill through tall dry grass with black patches of old burn scars lined up under a line of wire poles. A tilted last fence, a lone boulder shielding a small grassy hollow. Warm light turning paler, dust glowing in sunbeams, storm front nearer on the upper left, colossal hooped oak on the far ridge.
```
**Зона 2 — Присяжный камень (концепт):**
```
Concept art, environment keyframe. A small round plateau on a mountain ledge. In the center a flat disc-shaped boulder polished smooth by hands, with two small carved hollows the size of a knife hilt. Beside it a low round grassy burial mound taller than a man, untouched, a footpath circling it at a distance. Three pale faded old figures standing near the stone. A cliff drops on one side, a rope ladder pulled up at its edge. Warm but paler light, the mound casting a long shadow over the stone. Solemn, silent.
```
**Зона 3 — Стрелокопни (концепт):**
```
Concept art, environment keyframe. A mountain slope pitted with a dozen digging craters and sand spoil heaps, like a field sown by lightning. Branching glassy tubes of fused sand and smooth dark stone fingers sticking out of the ground in clusters, some laid in a chain running downhill. Two small sod-roofed pole shelters with sieves, sacks and shovels. An upper open path along the slope and a deep lower trench. Wire poles with iron-and-sinew cables, scorched rings on the ground. Ochre light fading, grey-blue sky, deep but not black shadows in the pits. Silent labor, unease.
```
**Зона 4 — Ведёрный ряд (концепт):**
```
Concept art, environment keyframe. A long narrow mountain pasture shelf. A row of eight small log wells every twenty meters, each with a tall well sweep and an empty cracked wooden bucket on a chain, forming a rhythm of diagonals. A goat pen of poles, two grey log sheds. To one side a steep slope, to the other a cliff overlooking warm lit diggings far below. Cool diffuse grey light, frequent pale blue lightning flashes outlining the sweeps. Empty, ringing, lonely.
```
**Зона 5 — Кумирная горка (концепт, арена):**
```
Concept art, environment keyframe, boss arena. A flat round hilltop forty meters across, slightly bowl-shaped, short burnt grass and scorched patches increasing toward the center. In the center a shallow pit where an idol once stood and a charred black oak stump with remnants of carving under the char, no embers, no smoke. Three low weathered stones at the edge, one with a worn thunder rosette. No cover. Heavy low clouds, lightning as the main light, ground lighter than silhouettes. Nearby below, a small flat altar stone with a steady silver moonlight glow in its cup. Judgement, emptiness where a god stood.
```
**Зона 6 — Громовой гребень (концепт):**
```
Concept art, environment keyframe. A knife-edge mountain ridge six meters wide above a sea of clouds, sky open on all sides, cold twilight with no warm light at all. At the top a colossal ancient oak, split many times and bound again and again with forged iron hoops of different ages, bark swelling in bulges between the hoops, half of the crown dead, vertical scorched grooves where lightning struck, glassy fused sand at the roots. A pale blue-violet lightning bolt striking the oak. Grey-blue clouds below, silhouettes darker than the sky. The edge of the world, vast and quiet.
```
**Портал/эпилог (концепт):**
